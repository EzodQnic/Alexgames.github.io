The game

What is The Auxano Project?

Auxanō is Greek for “I grow.” The game is about humanity outgrowing a world that has stopped making room for it.

Lillie is twenty-four, a physics PhD student who lives with her family in an old warehouse flat in Wapping, the Thames outside the window. A result on her bench refuses to go away, and it points somewhere impossible: a pair of boxes that share one place across any distance. To build it she will need the trust of the people around her, money she doesn't have, a datacentre in Iceland — and a mind bigger than her own.

Meanwhile the world has its own clock. Wealth piles up a little further every year, and the city shows it: bus lines are suspended, shops shutter, the streetlights go out. Every big thing Lillie does moves that clock. Exploring never does — take as long as you like.

“Everyone in this game wants something, and almost nobody tells you what. Learning the gap is the game.”

What's inside

Features

💬

Everyone Talks Back

Type what you want to say. Every character answers in their own voice, with a face that reacts — and remembers how you treated them.

🤝

Trust Is Earned

You can't talk your way past anyone. People open up to what you do: the favour, the gift, the evening you gave up for someone else.

🌆

A World That Changes

London and Reykjavík in hand-lit pixel art. As the world tilts, the same streets dim, shutter and empty — with no loading of a different map.

🧭

Choices That Close Doors

Some errands can't both be done. Nothing fails — but what you choose decides which of eight endings you're capable of reaching.

🚌

Go Places

Bus stops, flights and departure boards. The map only shows what someone has told you about — the world grows as you learn it.

✨

An Original Score

Every key character has their own theme, and each place its own tune.
