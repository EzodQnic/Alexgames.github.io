The game

What is Starbob?

Starbob is a love letter to the golden age of arcade games. Bounce and climb through 10 hand-crafted worlds — from a sunny farmstead to a neon city, pyramid dunes, a cloud citadel and a moonlit shrine — grabbing every star before the fuse burns out.

Time your jumps, ride the platforms, and outwit burrowing zombies, roaming slimes and swooping birds. Trap enemies in crystal with the P power-up, chase the flying extra-life, and push your streak on the inter-level score cards. Clear all ten worlds and the endless rounds begin — how far can you climb?
